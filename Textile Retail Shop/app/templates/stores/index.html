Financial year {{ year }} · e.g. {{ example }} Bill series

A till's bills are named after the storey it stands on: POS → floor → prefix → financial year → next number. The billing counter does the rest — a cashier never types a bill number and cannot change one.

Floors are created in the warehouse. The chain warehouse → store → floor → till is one master, kept in the Essa warehouse's Locations screen, and this shop mirrors it — the same way it mirrors the stores themselves. Rows marked warehouse below are read-only here; change them there and they follow through on the next sync. Anything you add on this page is marked local and is left alone by a sync — that is for a shop running on its own, with no warehouse to read.
